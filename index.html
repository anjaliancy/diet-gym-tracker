Level Up Execution Rules (Form & Progression Reminders)

1. Shoulder Protection Tuck elbows to 45° (Arrow shape, not 'T' shape) on Bench & Pressing. Neutral grip options protect joints.
2. Mid-Week Core & Active Recovery Day 3 burns extra calories without draining central nervous system energy, allowing upper/lower muscle groups to recover.
3. Double Progression Method When you hit top reps on all sets (e.g. 3 x 12 reps), increase weight next session and start back at 8 reps.
